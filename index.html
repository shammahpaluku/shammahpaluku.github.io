DICE Operations

Slicing, Dicing, Drilling, and Rolling operations on multidimensional data

-- DICE: Sales from specific region AND product category AND time period SELECT dr.region, dpt.product_type_name, SUM(fs.price * fs.quantity) AS total_sales FROM fact_sales fs JOIN dim_product dp ON fs.product_id = dp.product_id JOIN dim_product_type dpt ON dp.product_type_id = dpt.product_type_id JOIN dim_store ds ON fs.store_id = ds.store_id JOIN dim_city dc ON ds.city_id = dc.city_id JOIN dim_region dr ON dc.region_id = dr.region_id JOIN dim_time dt ON fs.time_id = dt.time_id WHERE dr.region = 'Great Lakes' AND dpt.product_type_name IN ('Electronics', 'Clothing') AND dt.action_date BETWEEN '2024-01-01' AND '2024-03-31' GROUP BY dr.region, dpt.product_type_name;
SLICE Operations

Reducing dimensionality by fixing one dimension

-- SLICE: Sales for a specific time period SELECT dt.action_date, SUM(fs.price * fs.quantity) AS daily_sales FROM fact_sales fs JOIN dim_time dt ON fs.time_id = dt.time_id WHERE dt.action_date BETWEEN '2024-01-01' AND '2024-12-31' GROUP BY dt.action_date ORDER BY dt.action_date;
ROLLUP Operations

Aggregating data along hierarchical dimensions

-- ROLLUP: Hierarchical aggregation by region SELECT COALESCE(dr.region_name, 'All Regions') as region, COALESCE(dc.city_name, 'All Cities') as city, SUM(fs.price * fs.quantity) as total_sales FROM fact_sales fs JOIN dim_store ds ON fs.store_id = ds.store_id JOIN dim_city dc ON ds.city_id = dc.city_id JOIN dim_region dr ON dc.region_id = dr.region_id GROUP BY ROLLUP(dr.region_name, dc.city_name);
